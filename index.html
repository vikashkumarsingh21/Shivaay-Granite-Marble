About Shivaay Granite & Marble

The Heart of Natural Stone Excellence in Morbi.

Strategically located in Morbi — India’s celebrated ceramics capital — on the bustling Rajkot-Morbi Highway, Shivaay Granite & Marble is your trusted destination for luxury natural stone and architectural tiles.

Whether you are building your dream villa, renovating a contemporary kitchen, or sourcing for commercial developments across Morbi, Rajkot, and Saurashtra, our expansive stockyard offers thousands of hand-selected slabs in pristine finishes. We combine competitive wholesale pricing with meticulous craftsmanship.

On-Site Precision Cutting

Bridge saw cutting and custom sink cutouts done right at our yard.

Fine Edge Polishing

Bullnose, chamfer, bevel, and bookmatched edge detailing.

Wholesale & Retail Pricing
